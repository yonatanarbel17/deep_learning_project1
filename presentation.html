Overcoming the Main Challenge

Problem: Data Leakage

  • Game-level split: only 200 val samples from 2 games
  • Augmented variants leaked across splits
  • 23-point overfit gap (95% train vs 72% val)

Solution: Frame-Group Split

  • Group by (game_id, frame) - all variants stay together
  • 595 val samples from 9 games
  • No overfitting - val ≥ train

Ablation Study

What Didn't Work

Other Backbones

ResNet18 too weak, ResNet50/Swin overfit. EfficientNet-B2 was the sweet spot.

Board-Level Transformer

Squares attending to each other added complexity without gains - overlap padding already provides context.

Full Fine-Tuning from Start

Catastrophic forgetting destroyed ImageNet features. 95% train vs 72% val.

Heavy Augmentation

Strong ColorJitter overlapped with offline variants, creating unrealistic images.

Key Takeaways

1

Data Quality > Model Complexity

Proper split and augmentation contributed more than any architecture change

2

Gradual Unfreezing is Key

Stabilize the head, then fine-tune everything - prevents catastrophic forgetting (+4%)

3

Right-Sized Models Win

With limited data, inductive bias matters more than raw capacity
